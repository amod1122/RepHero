# RepHero Product Context

## Product

RepHero provides trained remote professionals for healthcare practices in the United States. The website explains available roles, industries served, the engagement process, and estimated staffing savings.

## Audience and Primary Action

The site is for healthcare practice owners and managers evaluating remote staffing. Its primary conversion is booking a call; the booking form warns visitors not to submit protected health information.

## Site Capabilities

The Next.js App Router site includes service, industry, how-it-works, success stories, about, our-team, booking, privacy, and terms pages. The savings calculator is embedded directly in the homepage experience; the legacy calculator URL redirects to that section. Content and SEO metadata are maintained in typed files under `src/content`. The dedicated Our Team page currently contains temporary preview profiles pending final team information.

## Brand and Compliance

The brand is RepHero / RepHero Global LLC. Site copy describes HIPAA-trained staff and a Business Associate Agreement for engagements. The website itself states that it does not collect patient information or process PHI through its forms.

## Technical Context

The project uses Next.js, TypeScript, React, and Tailwind CSS. See `package.json` for available scripts and `src/content` for the maintained site content.
